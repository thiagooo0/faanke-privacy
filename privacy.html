FaankePrivacy Policy · 隐私政策 · プライバシーポリシー

Faanke has no server, no account and no analytics, and keeps nothing about you anywhere but your own device. Faanke for Mac never touches the network. Faanke for iPhone goes online for one optional feature, the weather, and then talks only to Apple.

What Faanke stores

Everything Faanke saves stays on your own Mac or iPhone, inside the app's own storage:

  • Session records — when each focus or rest period started, how long it ran and whether it ran to the end, for History (and, on iPhone, the tomatoes and flowers on the home screen).
  • Preferences — durations, how the clock or edge effect looks, your language, and on Mac the global shortcut and whether Faanke opens at login.
  • On iPhone: the effects you save, and resized copies of any photos you add to a photo-wall background.
  • On iPhone, if weather is on: the latest forecast and the place it was fetched for (see below).
  • On iPhone: whether you have unlocked Faanke Pro.

That's the complete list. No name, no email, no device identifier, no usage analytics, no crash reports. There is no server, so there is no copy of any of this anywhere else.

Faanke for Mac cannot connect to the internet

This is not just a promise. Faanke for Mac runs in the macOS App Sandbox and does not request the network entitlement (com.apple.security.network.client), so macOS itself blocks the app from opening outgoing connections.

Faanke for iPhone: weather and location

Weather is off until you turn it on, either as Home Weather (part of Faanke Pro) or as a weather background on the clock. When it is on:

  • Faanke asks for your approximate location only, and only while you are using the app. You can type in a city instead, which needs no location permission at all.
  • To get the forecast, those approximate coordinates are sent to Apple's WeatherKit service. To show your city's name, or to look up a city you typed, they go to Apple's geocoding service. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. Apple handles these requests under its own privacy policy.
  • The latest forecast and the coordinates it was fetched for are kept on your iPhone, so Faanke can tell when it actually needs a new one. They are overwritten by the next forecast and never leave the device.
  • Your location is used for weather and nothing else. It is not linked to your identity and is never used for tracking or advertising.

You can turn weather off in Faanke's settings, or withdraw location access at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services.

Photos (iPhone)

Photos for a photo-wall background are picked in Apple's own photo picker, so Faanke never gets access to your library — only to the photos you choose. It keeps a resized copy of each one inside the app, and deletes copies that no saved effect uses any more.

Notifications, Lock Screen and widget (iPhone)

The end-of-phase notification is scheduled on your iPhone itself, and the Lock Screen card and the widget are updated by the app on the device. None of them go through a server.

Purchases (iPhone)

Faanke Pro is bought through Apple's App Store, and payment is handled entirely by Apple: we never see your payment details or your Apple Account. Apple tells the app only whether Faanke Pro is unlocked, and the app remembers that in your iPhone's Keychain so it still knows when you are offline. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account settings.

Feedback

The feedback button opens an email in your own mail app, addressed to us and prefilled with the app version, iOS version, device model and language, so we can reproduce problems. You can read and change all of it before sending, and nothing is sent unless you send it. Your email address is used only to reply to you.

No third parties

Faanke contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no crash reporter and no tracking of any kind. Apart from the weather requests to Apple described above, nothing is shared with anyone, and nothing is ever sold.

Deleting your data

Mac: moving Faanke to the Trash removes its sandbox container along with every record and preference.

iPhone: deleting the app removes its records, effects, photos, settings and weather data. The one exception is the small “unlocked” flag in the Keychain, which iOS may keep after the app is deleted; it holds no personal information and never leaves the device. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, iOS includes Faanke's data in that backup, as it does for every app.

Faanke is suitable for all ages. It collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Was Faanke speichert

Alles, was Faanke speichert, bleibt auf deinem eigenen Mac oder iPhone, im Speicher der App:

  • Auf dem iPhone: deine gespeicherten Effekte und verkleinerte Kopien der Fotos, die du einer Fotowand hinzufügst.

Das ist die vollständige Liste. Kein Name, keine E-Mail-Adresse, keine Gerätekennung, keine Nutzungsanalyse, keine Absturzberichte. Es gibt keinen Server, also existiert davon nirgendwo sonst eine Kopie.

Das ist nicht bloß ein Versprechen. Faanke für den Mac läuft in der macOS App Sandbox und fordert die Netzwerkberechtigung (com.apple.security.network.client) nicht an — macOS selbst blockiert daher jede ausgehende Verbindung der App.

Faanke fürs iPhone: Wetter und Standort

Das Wetter ist aus, bis du es einschaltest — entweder als Wetter auf der Startseite (Teil von Faanke Pro) oder als Wetter-Hintergrund auf der Uhr. Wenn es eingeschaltet ist:

  • Faanke fragt nur nach deinem ungefähren Standort, und nur während du die App benutzt. Du kannst stattdessen auch eine Stadt eingeben; dafür ist gar keine Standortfreigabe nötig.
  • Um die Vorhersage abzurufen, werden diese ungefähren Koordinaten an Apples Dienst WeatherKit gesendet. Um den Namen deiner Stadt anzuzeigen oder eine eingegebene Stadt zu finden, gehen sie an Apples Geokodierungsdienst. An uns oder sonst jemanden wird nichts gesendet. Apple verarbeitet diese Anfragen nach seiner eigenen Datenschutzrichtlinie.
  • Die letzte Vorhersage und die Koordinaten, für die sie abgerufen wurde, bleiben auf deinem iPhone, damit Faanke erkennt, wann wirklich eine neue nötig ist. Sie werden von der nächsten Vorhersage überschrieben und verlassen das Gerät nie.
  • Dein Standort wird nur für das Wetter verwendet und für nichts anderes. Er wird nicht mit deiner Identität verknüpft und nie für Tracking oder Werbung genutzt.

Du kannst das Wetter in den Einstellungen von Faanke ausschalten oder den Standortzugriff jederzeit in den iOS-Einstellungen › Datenschutz & Sicherheit › Ortungsdienste entziehen.
